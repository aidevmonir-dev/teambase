The part every SaaS needs, built once and tested.

TeamBase is a starter for multi-tenant products: sign-up, organisations, roles, invitations, plans with limits, Stripe billing and an audit log. Django REST backend, Next.js frontend, one deployment.

Create an account

The demo signs you in to a sample company with a team and projects. It is deleted after 24 hours.

Organisations as tenants

Every record belongs to an organisation, and every query is scoped to the signed-in member's organisation. Users can belong to several and switch between them.

Roles that mean something

Owners handle billing and roles, admins manage the team, members do the work. Enforced on the server, tested in the suite.

Invitations and seat limits

Invite by email with a one-time link. Open invitations count against the plan's seats, so a team cannot outgrow its plan.

Plans and Stripe billing

Free, Pro and Business with seat and project limits. Stripe Checkout to upgrade, signed webhooks to confirm, a customer portal to manage.

Audit log

Who invited whom, who changed a role, who upgraded the plan. Visible to admins under Activity.

One deploy, one origin

Django serves the REST API and the exported Next.js app together. Session cookies with CSRF, no tokens in local storage.
